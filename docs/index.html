THE LINUX WORKSPACE

Make yourself
at home in Omarchy.

A preinstalled Arch Linux desktop with guided owner setup. Your Mac’s keyboard, clipboard, and shared files, connected to a world you can make your own.

  • Signed, verified factory image
  • Custom VirGL graphics through Metal
  • Dynamic display & guest integration
Explore Omarchy ↗O THE MACOS WORKSPACE

A fresh Mac.
Without another Mac.

Try software, verify a system change, or keep a workflow separate. Create a virtual Mac from an Apple restore image, using Apple’s native virtualization.

  • Apple restore-image compatibility checks
  • Your choice of CPU, memory & storage
  • Independent workspaces & file snapshots

Let the GPU work.
